Welcome to the website (the “Site”) operated by Go-Uplift LTD (“Go-Uplift LTD,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). We provide this Site to share information about Go-Uplift LTD and its fundraising offerings, mobile applications, and associated services (collectively, together with the Site, the “Services”). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personally identifiable information (“Personal Data”) and other information obtained from people who visit or use the Site and Services (“you” or “your”).

## Information We Collect

When you engage with our Services, we may obtain Personal Data and other information in the ways outlined below.

### Personal Data That You Provide Through the Services

We receive Personal Data that you choose to submit, including when you send an enquiry, complete a survey, sign up to access the Services, or use particular features. Where we request Personal Data, we aim to make this Privacy Policy accessible.

When you willingly submit Personal Data, you consent to its handling as described in this Privacy Policy. Information provided through the Services may be transferred from your location to Go-Uplift LTD offices, servers, and authorised third parties in Kenya, Ireland and Switzerland.

### Other Information

As you use Go-Uplift LTD Services, we may automatically obtain and retain information that does not directly identify you. This information may be collected through different technologies and held by Go-Uplift LTD or in systems managed by its affiliates, representatives, or service providers. We may combine it with other non-identifying information to understand, for example, total Site traffic, visits to individual pages, and the internet service provider domains used by visitors. Personal Data is not used or made available for these measurements.

### Cookies

We may use small data files known as cookies, which are sent to your browser by the computer hosting the Services. Cookies can support additional Site functions and help us assess how the Services are used. For example, they may allow you to navigate the Site without repeatedly entering your password during the same visit. We do not use cookies to collect Personal Data without your permission. Your browser’s help menu explains how to receive cookie alerts or disable cookies. Keeping cookies enabled may be necessary to benefit from certain Service features.

### Aggregated Personal Data

To understand users and improve our Services, Go-Uplift LTD may analyse demographic information, interests, and behaviour using Personal Data and other information provided to us. We may prepare combined, non-identifying findings and share them with affiliates, agents, business partners, or other third parties for lawful purposes. Such reports may also be used to explain our Services to existing or potential partners. These aggregated findings do not identify individuals.

### Location Information

Certain features may use location data, such as GPS information from your mobile device. If you enable location-based features, your location may appear publicly within the Services. Other users may view, share, or misuse this information beyond our control and without your knowledge. Consider these risks before enabling location sharing. We may also analyse location information in aggregated form as explained above.

### Google Analytics

Go-Uplift LTD may authorise third-party service providers to use cookies or similar technologies to collect information about your browsing activities over time and across different websites following your use of our Services. For example, we use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google Inc. (“Google”). Google Analytics uses cookies to help us understand how visitors interact with our Site, analyse usage patterns and enhance your experience when using our Services.

For more information about how Google uses this data, please visit:  
https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/partners/
