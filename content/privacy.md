# Privacy Policy

Go-Uplift LTD (“Go-Uplift LTD,” “GoUplift,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) runs a fundraising platform for Kenya, made up of the website at gouplift.africa and its related applications and services (together, the “Services”). This Privacy Policy explains what personally identifiable information (“Personal Data”) we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what rights you have over it. Personal Data means information that identifies you or can be used to identify you.

We are the “data controller” for the Personal Data described in this policy. That means we decide how and why it is used. We handle it in line with the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya and the regulations made under it.

## Registration with the Data Protection Commissioner

We are in the process of registering Go-Uplift LTD with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) of Kenya as a data controller, as the Data Protection Act requires. We will publish our registration number here once the certificate has been issued. You can find out more about the ODPC at https://www.odpc.go.ke.

## Information We Collect

We collect only what we need for the purposes set out in this policy. What we collect depends on how you use the Services.

### If You Visit the Services

- Technical information that your browser and device send us, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you visit, and the time of your visit.
- Cookies, described below.

### If You Create an Account

- Your name, email address, and password. We store your password in a protected form, not as plain text.
- Optionally, a username, phone number, profile photo, a short bio, your location, your website, and other profile details you choose to add. Anything you add to a public profile can be seen by others.
- If you sign in with Google, the name, email address, and profile photo that Google shares with us.

### If You Donate

- Your name (which you can choose to keep anonymous) and your email address. We use your email address to send you a receipt.
- For M-Pesa and Airtel Money payments, your mobile number.
- The amount, the date, the payment method, the payment reference or transaction code, and the message you write, if any.
- We do not receive or store your card number, your M-Pesa or Airtel Money PIN, or your PayPal password. Those are handled by SasaPay and PayPal.

### If You Raise Money

- The details of your fundraiser: its title, story, pictures, the amount you want to raise, who it is for, and its dates.
- To verify you before you are paid out (see “Verification and Compliance” below): your legal name, your national ID or passport number and a photograph of the document, a Kenyan mobile number, the country you live in, how much you expect to raise and over what period, whether you or someone close to you holds a public position, your KRA PIN and certificate, evidence supporting your fundraiser (such as invoices or letters), and proof of your payout account.
- Payout details: the M-Pesa number, or the bank name and account number, where you want to be paid, the name on that account, and, if the account belongs to someone else, why.
- Your withdrawal requests and what happened to them.

### If You Act for an Organisation

- The organisation’s details and registration documents.
- Information about the people who own or control it: their names, ID type and number, share of ownership, role, country of residence, and whether they hold a public position.
- The organisation’s members and payout accounts and, if you use our notification or developer tools, the web addresses you ask us to send notifications to.

### If You Sign Up for Our Newsletter

- Your email address and the date you signed up.

### If You Offer Your Message as a Testimonial

- The message you wrote when you donated. If you tick the box to offer it, our team reads it before it can appear on our website. If it is approved, it is shown with your first name and the initial of your last name. You can withdraw it at any time from your profile.

### Information From Our Checks and From How the Services Are Used

- Records of our verification, risk assessment, and review decisions, and of which member of our team handled them.
- Emails and messages between you and us, including receipts, password resets, and replies to your questions.
- Information we need to prevent abuse, such as how many donation attempts have been made from an email address or an IP address in a short time.

### Cookies and Analytics

Cookies are small data files that the website puts in your browser. We use cookies that are needed to keep you signed in and your account secure. We do not use advertising cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings, but you may then be unable to sign in.

We use Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to count visits and measure how quickly pages load. We use them for overall figures, not to look at what any one person did. You can read how Vercel handles data at https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

### Location

We do not collect your device’s location. A location you type into your profile or fundraiser is shown as you entered it.

### Aggregated Information

We may combine information into totals and statistics that do not identify anyone, such as the amount raised on the platform or the number of donations, and use or share them, for example on our website or to explain our Services to partners.

## Why We Use Your Personal Data

- **To provide the Services you ask for.** To create your account, let you start a fundraiser, take and record donations, send receipts, and review and pay withdrawals. We do this because it is necessary to carry out our agreement with you.
- **To meet our legal duties.** To verify identity, prevent fraud, money laundering, and terrorist financing, keep records, and respond to lawful requests from authorities.
- **To keep the Services safe and working.** To prevent abuse, fix problems, and understand how the Services are used so that we can improve them. We do this because it is in our legitimate interests, and yours, to have a secure and reliable service.
- **To contact you.** About your account, your donations, your fundraisers, and your withdrawals, and, if you have signed up, to send you our newsletter.
- **Where you have chosen it.** For example, if you sign up for the newsletter or offer your message as a testimonial. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

We do not sell your Personal Data.

### Automated Checks

Some rules run automatically, such as the limits on how much can be paid out at each verification level and the limits on repeated donation attempts. We also rate the risk of fundraisers using fixed rules, to help decide which ones need a closer look. A member of our team reviews every verification and every withdrawal. A decision about you is never made by a computer alone.

## Verification and Compliance

Before we pay money out to a fundraiser, we verify who they are. We check the identity documents and details they give us, and we may check names against lists of politically exposed persons and sanctioned persons. We keep records of these checks. We do this to protect donors and to meet our legal duties, including under Kenya’s anti-money laundering laws. We may report suspicious activity to the authorities where the law requires it.

## Who We Share Personal Data With

We share Personal Data only where it is needed for the purposes above.

- **Payment partners.** SasaPay processes payments in Kenyan shillings (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, SasaPay wallet, and card) and PayPal processes payments in US dollars. They receive the details needed to take and record the payment, and they handle them under their own privacy policies.
- **Organisations.** We do not publish your donation message. If you donate to a fundraiser that belongs to a verified organisation that has set up notifications, we tell that organisation the amount and the date and, unless you chose to give anonymously, your name and your message. We do not give it your email address or your phone number.
- **Service providers.** Companies that help us run the Services, only for that purpose and under their own obligations to protect the data: hosting and database services (Convex and Vercel), email delivery (Resend), sign-in (Google), and website analytics (Vercel).
- **Our team.** Access to verification documents is limited to the members of our team who review them, and their reviews are recorded.
- **Authorities and legal requirements.** We may release Personal Data when legally required or when we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to (i) meet a legal duty, (ii) safeguard Go-Uplift LTD’s rights or property, (iii) respond to urgent threats to users or public safety, or (iv) protect against legal liability.
- **Business transfers.** If Go-Uplift LTD buys or sells a business or assets, or is involved in a merger, restructuring, or similar transaction, Personal Data may be included among the assets transferred.

## Transfers Outside Kenya

Some of the providers above store or process Personal Data in other countries, which may include countries in the European Union and the United States. We transfer Personal Data outside Kenya only where the Data Protection Act allows it, for example where it is needed to provide the Services you asked for, or where the provider is bound to protect the data appropriately. If you would like to know more, please contact us.

## How Long We Keep Personal Data

We keep Personal Data only for as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy.

- Account and fundraiser information: while your account is open, and for a reasonable time afterwards.
- Verification, risk, and payout records: for as long as the law requires, which can be several years after you stop using the Services.
- Newsletter sign-ups: until you ask us to remove you.

When we no longer need Personal Data, and the law does not require us to keep it, we delete it or make it anonymous.

## Your Rights

Under the Data Protection Act you have the right to:

- be told how your Personal Data is used, which this policy does;
- ask for access to the Personal Data we hold about you;
- object to us processing all or part of your Personal Data;
- ask us to correct Personal Data that is false or misleading; and
- ask us to delete Personal Data that is false or misleading, and other Personal Data we no longer need.

You can also withdraw any consent you have given us, for example to receive our newsletter or to show your message as a testimonial.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm who you are first. Some records, such as verification and payout records, we must keep by law even if you ask us to delete them. If that applies, we will tell you.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your Personal Data, please contact us first so that we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, at https://www.odpc.go.ke.

## What Happens If You Do Not Give Us Information

You can browse the Services, and donate, without creating an account. Giving us your Personal Data is otherwise your choice, but some things depend on it:

- To receive money, you must be verified. We need to know who we are paying, to protect donors and to meet our legal duties, so we cannot pay you out if you do not give us the verification information we ask for.
- If you donate without giving an email address, we cannot send you a receipt.
- If you do not give the details a payment needs, such as a mobile number for M-Pesa, we cannot take the payment.

## Children

The Services are for people who are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us Personal Data, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Links to Other Web Sites

This Privacy Policy applies to our Services only. Links within the Services may lead to websites that Go-Uplift LTD neither owns nor controls (“Third Party Sites”). Our practices do not extend to those sites, and providing a link does not mean we have reviewed or endorsed them. Please consult each third-party website directly about its privacy practices.

## Signing In With Google

You can sign in to the Services with your Google account. When you do, Google shares your name, email address, and profile photo with us, and we use them to create or sign you in to your account. Google’s use of your information is governed by Google’s own privacy policy. We are not responsible for Google’s privacy practices.

## Security

We take reasonable steps to protect Personal Data against loss, misuse, unauthorised access or disclosure, alteration, and destruction. These include encrypted connections to the Services, storing passwords in a protected form, limiting who on our team can see verification documents and recording their reviews, and limiting repeated attempts. Nevertheless, no transmission over the internet or by email can be guaranteed to be entirely secure or free from errors. Email correspondence in particular may not be secure, so please consider carefully what Personal Data you send to us by email.

If a security incident affects your Personal Data and is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the Data Protection Commissioner, as the law requires.

## Exclusions

This Privacy Policy covers Personal Data collected through the Services and does not extend to information Go-Uplift LTD obtains through other channels. It also does not govern unsolicited material you send to Go-Uplift LTD through the Services or otherwise. Such material may include public forum posts, suggestions for new products or changes to existing products, and other unsolicited contributions (together, “Unsolicited Information”). Unsolicited Information is treated as non-confidential and may be copied, used, disclosed, or distributed by Go-Uplift LTD without restriction or attribution. This does not limit your rights over your Personal Data under this policy.

If we make significant changes, we will tell you through the Services or by email, and we will update the date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review the policy regularly, especially before sharing Personal Data.
